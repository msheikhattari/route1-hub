## 2. The curriculum in one page
**When Helping Hurts (Corbett & Fikkert).** Poverty = four broken relationships (God, self, others, creation) → poverty of spiritual intimacy / being / community / stewardship. The harm equation (p. 64): *material definition of poverty + god-complexes of the non-poor + feelings of inferiority of the poor = harm to both*. Relief vs rehabilitation vs development; "the biggest mistake by far is applying relief where development is needed." Avoid paternalism (resource, spiritual, knowledge, labor, managerial). Asset-based, participatory; "we are not bringing Christ to poor communities." Ch 10: triggers, supportive community, "start small, start soon, succeed." Ch 11: partner, don't clone; the "Donald Trump effect." Final word: repentance. **Keep the Oct 1 Indonesia exercise answers** — revisited Dec 3.
**Trafficking application:** the survivor-led literature independently says the same thing — drop "rescue" language (ACF Guiding Principles; Polaris survivors), don't do for survivors what they can do, partner with existing experts rather than start a shelter (Nazarene guide; Safe House Project's own church post), beware spiritual paternalism (Knight et al. 2026, *Religions*: survivors report coercive faith-based care). Raleigh Sadler's *Vulnerable* (B&H 2019) is the best bridge book: "theology of glory" (rescue moment) vs "theology of the cross" (long recovery).
**Critiques to hold:** structural drivers (demand, housing, foster care, immigration law) are under-weighted; help conditioned on religious participation is experienced as coercion.

**God's Heart to Eradicate Trafficking (Safe House Project × RightNow Media, 2023).** ~1 hr of video total; short prompts + lesson guides. Anchor text Matt 16:13-20 (Caesarea Philippi, "gates of Hades"). Register: evangelical/charismatic, revival + spiritual-warfare framing. L2 (Dunn) is the one "realities" lesson — OnWatch supplies the identification content. **Cautions:** SHP's headline stats ("300,000 children," "<1% identified," "80% re-trafficked") are advocacy estimates researchers dispute; SHP took a political position against Polaris/the hotline in Feb 2025; SHP is Charity Navigator 4-star but not ECFA. SHP is credible on services (6,800+ survivors since 2020; co-leads Frederick County MD task force).

## 5. Numbers & myths (what to actually say)
- US legal definition (TVPA): sex trafficking by force/fraud/coercion OR any minor in commercial sex; labor by force/fraud/coercion. No movement required.
- **No credible national prevalence estimate exists** (CRS 2025). Hotline = reports, not prevalence. **Maryland 2025 hotline:** 386 signals, 175 cases, 259 victims (87 sex / 37 labor / 22 both; 35 minors). Top MD sex venues: residence-based, hotel/motel, illicit massage; top labor: domestic work.
- **Recruiters are known people:** family 41.5% and intimate partner 39.3% of sex-trafficking recruiters (Polaris 2020). Only 11% of federal sex-trafficking victims documented as recruited online (HTI 2023) — Snapchat #1.
- **Federal cases are ~98% sex trafficking** but labor trafficking is systematically under-identified (TIP 2025 priority rec). Maryland labor: Eastern Shore H-2B crab pickers (CDM reports).
- **Hotels:** MD law (Bus. Reg. §15-210) requires annual staff training + certification; **only 82 of 765 properties certified in 2025; no penalty.** Howard County's motels are a corridor hotspot.
- **Myths to retire:** zip ties/white vans (0 cases in hotline data), Super Bowl surge (no evidence), "300,000/100,000 children" (an at-risk estimate, not a count), *Sound of Freedom*/OUR (Ballard misconduct; stranger-abduction framing). Polaris Myths & Facts.
- **Vet-a-statistic checklist:** primary source? what is counted (signals / detected / modeled / at-risk)? year + definition? denominator + method? round "000" number? rise = reality or reporting rule? emotionally perfect → more scrutiny.
- **Evidence on what works is thin:** school prevention improves knowledge (Love146 eval 2025); Safe Harbor cut juvenile arrests but services lag; survivor services under-evaluated; raids/"rescue" ops and over-policing harm survivors (62% of survivors arrested, 80% while trafficked — Polaris NSS). Funding disruption 2025: $88M lapse, VOCA cuts — local referral network in flux.
