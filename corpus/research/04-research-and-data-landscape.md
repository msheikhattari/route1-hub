# Human Trafficking: Research & Data Landscape for a Howard County, MD Faith-Based Group (as of Oct 2026)
(compiled 2026-10-01 by Claude research agent; "contested"/"modeled" labels preserved)

**Three recent changes most volunteer groups still get wrong:** (1) Polaris no longer runs the National Human Trafficking Hotline — HHS re-awarded it to Compass Connections, handover completed Dec 8, 2025; number unchanged (1-888-373-7888 / text 233733); treat 2025–26 data as a new series. (2) Maryland DOES now have a Safe Harbor law (Ch. 686/687, eff. Oct 1, 2023) — older fact sheets say it doesn't. (3) Federal victim-services funding was severely disrupted in 2025 (grant pause, cancellations, $88M lapse) — matters for any local referral map.

## 1. Scale and definitions
**US legal definition** — TVPA 2000, 22 U.S.C. § 7102(11): sex trafficking induced by force, fraud, or coercion, OR in which the person is under 18; labor/services obtained by force, fraud, or coercion. Minors in commercial sex need no force/fraud/coercion. No movement required. https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/22/7102 . Maryland: Crim. Law § 11-303 (human trafficking), § 3-1102 (labor trafficking). Most US prosecutions happen under state law.

**Global estimates & limits**
- ILO/Walk Free/IOM *Global Estimates of Modern Slavery* (Sept 2022, ref. year 2021): ~50M in modern slavery — 27.6M forced labour (incl. 6.3M forced commercial sexual exploitation) + 22M forced marriage. https://www.ilo.org/resource/news/50-million-people-worldwide-modern-slavery-0 . Caveat: surveys couldn't directly estimate adult forced sexual exploitation or child forced labour — extrapolated. "Modern slavery" ≠ US legal "trafficking."
- Walk Free *Global Slavery Index 2023* US: 1,091,000 (3.3 per 1,000) — MODELED, no US household survey; cite only as "a modeled estimate." https://cdn.walkfree.org/content/uploads/2023/11/14130741/gsi-country-study-usa.pdf
- UNODC GLOTIP 2024: detected victims +25% in 2022 vs 2019; children ~38% of detected. Detected ≠ prevalence.

**US hotline data**
- 2024 (last full Polaris year): 32,309 substantive signals; 11,999 situations; 21,865 victims; 65% sex / 22% labor / 13% both. https://polarisproject.org/blog/2025/06/the-2024-hotline-data-is-here-and-it-tells-a-story-of-hope-resilience-and-action/
- 2025 (Compass Connections): 52,664 signals; 15,085 cases; 21,457 victims; top sex venues: illicit massage/spa 649, residence-based 632, hotel/motel 490; top labor: domestic work 617. https://humantraffickinghotline.org/en/statistics . Transition year — counting conventions may differ.
- **MARYLAND 2025:** 386 signals (165 from victims/survivors); 175 cases; 259 victims; 87 sex / 37 labor / 22 both; 118 adults, 35 minors; 133 female, 39 male. Top sex venues: residence-based (13), hotel/motel (9), illicit massage (5); top labor: domestic work (11). 2024: 358 signals, 179 cases, 254 victims. All-time 2007–2025: 7,820 signals, 2,185 cases, 3,848 victims. https://humantraffickinghotline.org/en/statistics/maryland
- Operator change: https://acf.gov/otip/faq/national-human-trafficking-hotline-transition ; Polaris statement https://polarisproject.org/press-releases/polaris-issues-statement-on-the-future-of-the-national-human-trafficking-hotline/ ; followed congressional allegations of under-reporting tips to law enforcement (Grassley).

**Federal prosecution data**
- HTI *2023 Federal Human Trafficking Report* (June 2024): 202 new federal cases — 98% sex (197), 2% forced labor (5); 289 defendants; 670 victims; 48% of sex-trafficking defendants charged only re minors; 91 buyer-defendants (35%); 94% of forced-labor victims foreign nationals vs 3% of sex-trafficking victims; only 11% of sex-trafficking victims documented as recruited online (Snapchat 28, Facebook 10, Instagram 7); hotels most common arrest location. https://traffickinginstitute.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/2023-Federal-Human-Trafficking-Report-WEB-Spreads-LR.pdf ; data portal incl. MD district https://data.traffickinginstitute.org/
- BJS *Human Trafficking Data Collection Activities, 2025* (Jan 2026): FY2023 — 2,329 suspects referred, 1,213 prosecuted, 1,008 convicted (-9.8%); FBI NIBRS 2022 ~2,950 incidents, 81% sex. Defendants 92% male, 63% white, 96% US citizens. https://bjs.ojp.gov/document/htdca25.pdf
- CRS R47211 (Mar 2025): NO credible national prevalence estimate exists. https://www.congress.gov/crs_external_products/R/PDF/R47211/R47211.3.pdf
- DHS CCHT FY2024: HSI 1,686 cases, 2,545 arrests, 405 convictions; T status to 3,786 victims. https://www.dhs.gov/sites/default/files/2025-08/25_00809_ccht_fy24-year-in-review-annual-report_508.pdf
- **TIP Report 2025** (Sept 29, 2025): US Tier 1 but "decrease in federal prosecutions and significant decrease in convictions" — DOJ 146 prosecutions (181 FY23), 210 convictions (289). T-visa approvals doubled but median processing 14.9 months. GAO: 84% of H-2A investigations found violations, employers face 3% chance of investigation. https://www.state.gov/reports/2025-trafficking-in-persons-report/united-states/

## 2. How trafficking actually happens in the US
**Who recruits — mostly people the victim knows.** Polaris 2020 data: of known recruiters, 31.5% family/caregiver, 26.7% intimate partner, 28.2% employer. In SEX trafficking: family 41.5%, intimate partner 39.3%. Top tactics: job offer (35%), intimate-partner/marriage proposition (23%). https://polarisproject.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/Polaris-Analysis-of-2020-Data-from-the-National-Human-Trafficking-Hotline.pdf
- **Familial trafficking:** Polaris *In Harm's Way* (2023, National Survivor Study n=457): ~37–41% of child trafficking involves family. NSS: 62% of survivors had been arrested, 80% of those WHILE being trafficked; 96% prior abuse. https://polarisproject.org/national-survivor-study-findings-and-resources/
- Polaris Typology of 25 business models (2017): https://polarisproject.org/the-typology-of-modern-slavery/

**Online recruitment, gaming, sextortion.** Polaris 2020: internet 72% of known recruitment locations. HTI 2023: Snapchat top platform. **Financial sextortion is fastest-growing online threat to teens but usually NOT trafficking legally.** NCMEC: ~137 reports/day in 2025 (+37%); 90% of victims boys 14–17; 3 dozen+ teen suicides since 2021. 2025 CyberTipline 21.3M reports; child sex trafficking reports +323% (largely REPORT Act mandatory reporting, not a real surge). https://www.missingkids.org/gethelpnow/cybertipline/cybertiplinedata ; FBI https://www.fbi.gov/news/stories/the-financially-motivated-sextortion-threat

**Foster care, runaways, LGBTQ youth.** NCMEC: of ~27,000 endangered runaways 2024, ~1 in 7 likely CSEC victims; 17% of children missing from child welfare in 2025. https://www.missingkids.org/blog/2025/traffickers-target-kids-who-run-away . HHS/OPRE 2020: 19% of foster-youth runaway reports assessed as likely sex-trafficking. Urban Institute 2015 NYC LGBTQ youth: most engaged in survival sex; a MINORITY third-party trafficked — key messaging distinction. Center for Court Innovation/NIJ 2016: ~9,000–10,000 minors nationally — the only empirically grounded figure. https://www.innovatingjustice.org/publications/youth-involvement-sex-trade-national-study

**Labor trafficking & immigrants.** Urban Institute 2014: 71% of labor-trafficking victims entered on LAWFUL visas; avg $6,150 recruitment fees. Polaris 2022: 72% of hotline labor victims with known status held H-2A/H-2B/J-1/A-3/G-5. **Maryland Eastern Shore crab industry:** CDM *Picked Apart* (2010), *Breaking the Shell* (2020) — H-2B women crab pickers, 100% paid illegal recruitment fees. https://cdmigrante.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/02/PickedApart.pdf

**Illicit massage businesses (IMBs).** Polaris 2018: ~9,000 IMBs, ~$2.5B/yr; victims typically women 35–55 from China/S. Korea controlled by debt, isolation, shame. https://polarisproject.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/Human-Trafficking-in-Illicit-Massage-Businesses.pdf . Heyrick Research (now "The Network") ~10,058 IMBs. No county-level MD count exists; Howard County's 2025 report says IMB investigations rose late 2025. **Critique:** The Appeal (2018) — IMB crackdowns displace rather than help Asian workers; Red Canary Song. https://theappeal.org/a-national-campaign-to-crack-down-on-massage-businesses-may-harm-the-women-it-wants-to-help/

**Hotels/motels.** Polaris: 3,596 hotline cases involved hotels (81% escort-model). **MARYLAND LAW** Bus. Reg. § 15-210 (eff. Oct 1, 2022) requires annual anti-trafficking training for all lodging employees + certification to MD Dept. of Labor — Baltimore Sun (Sept 1, 2026): only 82 of 765 properties certified in 2025 (92 in 2023, 214 in 2024); NO PENALTY. https://www.baltimoresun.com/2026/09/01/human-trafficking-training-hotels/ → READY-MADE LOCAL PROJECT.

**Buyers/demand.** Demand Abolition *Who Buys Sex?* (2018, n=8,201): 6.2% bought in past year, 20.6% ever; ~25% of active buyers = ~75% of transactions. https://www.demandabolition.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/Demand-Buyer-Report-July-2019.pdf . Shively/NIJ 2012: SF john school cut 1-yr re-arrest 8.8%→4.5%; most demand programs unevaluated. HTI: 35% of 2023 federal sex-trafficking defendants were buyers.

**Truck stops.** TAT: ~2,782 trucker calls → 715 likely cases. MD 2019 HB 5 requires CDL schools to teach recognition. Howard County council does I-95 Welcome Center outreach.

## 3. What works, what doesn't, the "rescue" critique
**Evidence of benefit (modest but real)**
- School prevention: Love146 *Not a Number* — Jones, O'Brien & Graham, JIV 2025 (n=449): gains in rejecting victim-blaming, help-seeking efficacy, hotline awareness; NO effect on online-safety behaviors. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13139675/ . 3Strands PROTECT (CYSR 2026): staff ability to identify traffickers 22%→86% — knowledge only.
- **Maryland school mandate NOT yet law:** HB 763 (2025) died in Senate; HB 355 (2026) passed House 127–5 Mar 13, 2026, in Senate EEE at last check — VERIFY. https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/mgawebsite/Legislation/Details/hb0355?ys=2026RS
- Safe Harbor: Gies et al. 2020 — reduced juvenile prostitution arrests but services "not become a reality." Maryland: HB 827 (2019) created Regional Navigator Program; full Safe Harbor SB 292/HB 297 Ch. 686/687 eff. Oct 1, 2023.
- Vacatur: MD 2011 law expanded by True Freedom Act (HB 242, 2020) to labor trafficking.
- Survivor services: Dell et al. 2019 systematic review — evidence base thin; trauma-informed, survivor-directed, long-term housing/economic support = consensus but under-evaluated. FNUSA Standards of Care https://freedomnetworkusa.org/app/uploads/2019/07/Freedom-Network-Standards-of-Care-White-Paper-July-2019.pdf
- Awareness: DHS Blue Campaign eval 2023 — knowledge gains; behavior not yet measured.
- Hotel training: no outcome evaluation; MD compliance data is best local evidence.

**What doesn't work / backfires**
- Raids/"rescue" ops: FNUSA — misidentification, profiling, arrests of the people they claim to help. Yale GHJP: NYC Human Trafficking Intervention Courts — only ~45% met federal definition. https://law.yale.edu/ghjp/projects/gender-sexuality-and-rights/reports-prostitution-diversion-programs
- Over-policing: NSS 62% arrested, mostly while trafficked; TIP 2025: "victims continued to be arrested."
- Sensationalism: Martin & Hill, *Anti-Trafficking Review* 2019 — 76% of US print coverage pushed Super Bowl narrative despite no evidence.

**"Rescue industry" critique, stated fairly.** Agustín *Sex at the Margins* (2007); Bernstein "carceral feminism" (*Brokered Subjects* 2018); Zimmerman *Other Dreams of Freedom* (Oxford 2013) on how Protestant sexual ethics shaped US policy and over-weighted sex over labor trafficking. They don't deny coercion; they argue enforcement-first models harm the exploited and labor trafficking is neglected. Churches can honor the critique by prioritizing services, labor rights, survivor leadership over stings.

**Survivor leadership.** National Survivor Network https://nationalsurvivornetwork.org/ ; Survivor Alliance https://www.survivoralliance.org/ ; FNUSA white paper. Howard County appointed its first survivor leader to its council in 2025.

**Faith-based engagement.** Vanguard GCWJ *Ending Human Trafficking* podcast (Sandra Morgan, 300+ eps) https://endinghumantrafficking.org/ — model of evidence-literate faith engagement.

**2025 funding disruption.** FNUSA *Flying in the Face of Survivors* (Mar 2026): Jan 27, 2025 pause; 500+ DOJ grants cancelled April; 100+ trafficking grants ended Oct 1, 2025 with $88M unreleased; ~40% VOCA cut; immigrant survivors lost SNAP/Medicaid under July 2025 reconciliation law. https://freedomnetworkusa.org/app/uploads/2026/03/Flying-in-the-Face-of-Survivors-2025-FNUSA-HT-Policy-Report.pdf

## 4. Myths and misinformation
- Polaris Myths & Facts (12): https://polarisproject.org/myths-facts-and-statistics/
- Viral rumors (zip ties, white vans): Polaris 2015–2022 data — ZERO cases of zip ties as lures. https://polarisproject.org/human-trafficking-rumors/
- Super Bowl: GAATW *What's the Cost of a Rumour?* (2011) — no evidence. https://gaatw.org/publications/WhatstheCostofaRumour.11.15.2011.pdf
- "300,000 / 100,000 children": Estes & Weiner 2001 AT-RISK estimate (risk factors incl. living near a border); authors: "does not reflect the actual number of cases." UNH: "scientifically credible estimates do not exist." WaPo Fact Checker 2015 https://www.washingtonpost.com/news/fact-checker/wp/2015/09/02/the-fishy-claim-that-100000-children-in-the-united-states-are-in-the-sex-trade/
- Sound of Freedom / OUR: Vice documented exaggerated rescue claims; Ballard left OUR June 2023 after sexual-misconduct investigation; LDS Church called conduct "morally unacceptable"; six women's federal TVPRA suit (Oct 2024). https://www.vice.com/en/article/tim-ballards-departure-from-operation-underground-railroad-followed-sexual-misconduct-investigation/
- Wayfair/QAnon: Polaris July 2020 — hundreds of reports with nothing beyond the viral post; made it harder to help real callers.

**How to vet a statistic:** 1. Find the primary source, not the infographic. 2. What is counted: reports/signals, detected cases, modeled prevalence, or at-risk population? Never present one as another. 3. Check year and definition. 4. Look for denominator & method. 5. Prefer numbers with CIs or described samples over round "000" numbers. 6. Does a rise reflect reality or reporting rules? 7. If a claim is emotionally perfect for your cause, apply MORE scrutiny.

## 6. Research gaps a volunteer group could fill
1. **Hotel-training compliance audit (Howard County).** ~10% statewide compliance, no penalty. PIA-request MD Dept. of Labor certification list, map Howard County lodging (Route 1/I-95/BWI corridor), track year over year; offer free AHLA/ECPAT or Blue Campaign module to non-compliant properties. Nobody publishes this at county level.
2. **Local resource map and referral-gap tracker** — federal grant lapses put the network in flux; a dated directory (housing, legal/vacatur, immigration, language access Spanish/Chinese/Korean/Vietnamese) feeding the HTPCC's data tool.
3. **Legislative monitoring** — HB 355 (2026) status; HB 1 (2025 MDOT transit training); § 15-210 enforcement amendments; RNP appropriations; MHTTF 2026 priorities. Join the HTPCC Legislative Subcommittee.
4. **Labor-trafficking visibility** — partner with CDM/APALRC on know-your-rights outreach (domestic, restaurant, landscaping, construction) and document outcomes.
5. **Rumor-response protocol** — a short "we checked this" process for viral claims in congregations.
6. **Evaluate your own prevention work** — use Love146/PROTECT pre-post instruments.
7. **Public-comment presence at HTPCC meetings.**

Caveats: HTI's 2024 report and GOCPP navigator tables not yet parsed; UNODC page 404; state.gov partially inaccessible.
