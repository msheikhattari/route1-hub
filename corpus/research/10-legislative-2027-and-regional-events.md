## Testimony procedure (verified from 2026 committee FAQs)
Free MyMGA account → "Witness Signup." Oral sign-up and written testimony open **two business days before the hearing, 8 AM–6 PM**. Written = **PDF**, bill number at top, position (FAV/UNF/FWA), name/address/email/phone. Oral 2 minutes. JPR/EEE hear Tue–Thu 1 PM. For crossover bills House Judiciary takes oral only from sponsors but accepts written.
