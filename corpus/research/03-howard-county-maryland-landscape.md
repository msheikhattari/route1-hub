## A. Howard County Government and Law Enforcement

### Office of Human Trafficking Prevention (OHTP) and the Coordination Council (HTPCC)
- **What:** OHTP is the county's non-law-enforcement lead on sex and labor trafficking, created 2017 inside the **Department of Community Resources and Services (DCRS)**. It staffs the **Human Trafficking Prevention Coordination Council (HTPCC)**, first convened Dec 2017, made permanent by **CB 52-2019** (eff. Jan 2020). Lineage: 2014 county Human Trafficking Task Force (CR 8-2014).
- **Contact:** 9830 Patuxent Woods Dr, Columbia 21046. DCRS 410-313-6400; **OHTP 410-313-6558; ohtp@howardcountymd.gov**. Manager: **Ashton Petta** (apetta@howardcountymd.gov). https://www.howardcountymd.gov/human-trafficking-prevention
- **HTPCC composition (2025 report):** 19-23 members. Chair **Chuck Chandler** (business seat); Vice Chair **Fr. Travis K. Smith** (one of TWO faith-community seats; other is Vinitha Nithianandam). Also: Police Chief Gregory Der; State's Attorney Rich Gibson; DSS (Geneva Rieu); Health Officer designee (Ilise Marrazzo); HCPSS (Ivan Croft); Sheriff's rep; Commission for Women; HopeWorks (Ngozi Obineme); HoCo AGAST (Sara Cochran); survivor leader (Liz Kimbel); public members. TurnAround, Grassroots, HCGH seats "appointment pending." https://www.howardcountymd.gov/boards-commissions/human-trafficking-prevention-coordination-council
- **Meetings OPEN TO PUBLIC**, bi-monthly, 1-3 pm. Remaining 2026 date: **November 19, 2026** (typically 9830 Patuxent Woods Dr; Jan 2026 was at Elkridge 50+ Center, 6540 Washington Blvd). Apply for seats: https://www.howardcountymd.gov/applybc (Aug 2024 call sought two faith-based and two survivor reps).
- **Subcommittees (re-established 2025):** CORE Team (confidential case coordination: HCPD, TurnAround, HopeWorks, OHTP, Child Advocacy Center, CPS; met 6x in 2025); Legislative; **Training, Outreach and Prevention (TOP)**; Screening and Assessment Tool. 2026 priorities: countywide data-collection tool, standardized screening tool, social-media awareness, policy recs to County Executive. **Planning a council-led event for January 2027** — natural partnership point.
- **Key local data (HTPCC 2025 Annual Report, May 2026):** HCPD "recovered and offered services" to **35 new sex-trafficking victims in 2025** in hotel-based, residential and **illicit massage business (IMB)** settings, IMB investigations rising late in year. Direct-service providers served **48 new individuals**. HCPD victim-contact series: 2018: 12; 2019: 32; 2020: 17; 2021: 8; 2022: 25; 2023: 39. TurnAround served 50 trafficking victims in 2023. https://www.howardcountymd.gov/sites/default/files/2026-08/HTPCC.2025.AnnualReport.MAY2026.pdf ; 2024: https://www.howardcountymd.gov/sites/default/files/2026-02/HTPCC.2024.AnnualReport%20%281%29.pdf
- **Trainings:** OHTP gives **free in-person or virtual presentations** to community groups on request. 2025: TOP trained 300+ HCPSS student support staff, Corrections leadership, public-health nurses; 2024: AAPI groups, older adults, TAT briefing. County page links free courses (OVC, UMD SSW "Human Trafficking 101," Polaris); runs an **Allied Business Program**. Fliers in English, Spanish, Chinese, Korean, Vietnamese.
- **January events:** Jan 31, 2024 "Partner2Prevent" panel + *Shattered Dreams* discussion; Jan 2025 "Addressing the Vulnerabilities" resource fair with labor-trafficking HT 101, *Food Chains* screening, panel (HopeWorks, Grassroots, Luminus, Health Dept, Hon's Honey, Ayuda). No council-led Jan 2026 event in the 2025 report.
- **Federal grant history:** Fall 2020 OHTP + HopeWorks + HCPD Vice & Narcotics HT Section won a 3-year DOJ/OVC Enhanced Collaborative Model award (origin of CORE Team).

- **Howard County's Regional Navigator is TurnAround, Inc.** (also Baltimore County): Julie Harrison, jharrison@turnaroundinc.org; 24/7 443-279-0379; serves anyone **up to age 24** suspected/confirmed sex-trafficking victim; LE and DSS must notify. TurnAround runs a youth MDT case review in Howard County.

### Key Maryland laws
- 2007 reforms; **2011 vacatur** (CP §8-302), expanded by **True Freedom Act (2020)** to ~a dozen offenses + labor trafficking.
- **2019 Anti-Exploitation Act** (felony labor trafficking CL §3-1202) + Sex Trafficking Recodification (CL §3-1102; no "mistake of age" defense).
- **Safe Harbor SB 292/HB 297, eff. Oct 1, 2023:** child victims cannot be arrested/prosecuted for 26 qualifying offenses.
- **Hotel training mandate Bus. Reg. §15-210 (eff. Oct 1, 2022):** train every employee within 90 days, certify annually to Dept. of Labor; **no penalty**; only **82 of 765** properties certified 2025. Approved free trainings: PACT, BEST "Inhospitable to Human Trafficking," Marriott.
- **2025:** HB 1/SB 7 "See Someone, Save Someone Act" (Ch. 437; MDOT transit/airport/rest-stop training; certification due Jan 1, 2027). Expungement Reform Act (eff. Oct 1, 2025).
- **2026:** HB 771 health-occupations training (Ch. 205); HB 829/SB 473 rideshare driver training (Ch. 279/278); **HB 975 massage-therapy advertising** (Ch. 23, **eff. Oct 1, 2026**). **Failed:** HB 1348 (mandated reporters → navigators; passed House 133-0, died Senate JPR) and HB 355/SB 254. Likely 2027 re-introductions. https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/mgawebsite/Legislation/SubjectIndex/humant?ys=2026RS
